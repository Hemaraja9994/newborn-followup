No baby lost between the screen and the diagnosis

Most babies who are missed are not missed at the screen. They are lost afterwards, when a refer result is not followed up. This tracker shows who is due or overdue at each step of the 1-3-6 plan and writes a reminder for the family in their language.

The 1-3-6 plan

  • By 1 month: hearing screen (OAE and/or AABR). A refer on the first screen is followed by a rescreen.
  • By 3 months: full diagnostic audiological evaluation (including ABR) for any baby who does not pass the screen.
  • By 6 months: early intervention (hearing aids, family support, and referral as needed) for babies with confirmed hearing loss.
  • Risk factors: babies with risk factors who pass still need hearing monitoring. This tracker schedules a review by 9 months; some risk factors (for example congenital CMV) need earlier review.

Source: Joint Committee on Infant Hearing. Year 2019 Position Statement: Principles and guidelines for early hearing detection and intervention programs. J Early Hear Detect Interv 2019;4(2):1–44.
